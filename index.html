📖 Meal Selector Rules & Guidelines

Food Categories

Breakfast Proteins

  • Eggs (base)
  • Sausage (heavy)
  • Bacon (heavy)
  • Small steak (heavy)

Lean / Moderate Proteins

  • Grilled chicken (lean)
  • Smoked turkey breast (lean)
  • Tuna bowl (lean/moderate)
  • Tuna wrap (moderate)
  • Mahi (lean)
  • Burger plate (moderate / heavy with cheese)
  • NY strip (moderate)
  • Ribeye (heavy)
  • Country ribs (heavy)
  • Spaghetti and meatballs (heavy, contains carbs)
  • Carnivore chili (heavy)
  • Cheeseburger soup (heavy)

Sides / Add-ons

  • Green beans (default side)
  • Jalapeños (flavor add-on)
  • Carrots (optional grill add-on, controlled carb)
  • Coleslaw (occasional, controlled carb)
  • Peas (controlled carb)
  • Lima beans (controlled carb)
Rule 1: Breakfast Must Include Protein + Support
Breakfast should not be eggs alone unless necessary.
Rule 2: Every Meal Needs a Side or Add-On
A burger by itself is not a full selector result.

Exceptions: Carnivore chili can stand alone. Cheeseburger soup can stand alone.

Rule 3: Only One Heavy Meal Per Day ⚠️

Strictly enforced: Never two red-bordered (heavy) meals in the same day.

Rule 4: Tuna Frequency
Tuna 2–3x per week max. Don't pair tuna wrap with peas/lima beans.
Rule 5: Fish Frequency
No more than one fish-based meal per day (tuna bowl, tuna wrap, mahi).
Rule 6: Comfort Meal Rule
Carnivore chili or cheeseburger soup may replace dinner up to 3x per week. Never both on the same day.
Rule 7: Carb Control Rule
Only one controlled-carb item per meal (peas, lima beans, carrots, coleslaw, wheat wrap/bread).
Rule 8: Carrots Are Optional, Not Planned
Carrots are a grill add-on. Keep portion small/moderate.
Rule 9: Lean Correction Rule
If the previous meal was heavy, the next meal should be lean.
Rule 10: No Tuna Two Days in a Row
Tuna (bowl or wrap) should not appear on consecutive days.
Rule 11: Pantry Awareness
The generator only uses ingredients you've checked off in the 🛒 Pantry tab. Heavy meals to schedule are also picked there.
